Graduation Requirements:
A student must successfully complete all required courses and the minimum number of credit hours specified by the academic program before graduation.

Course Registration:
Students register courses according to their academic level, course prerequisites, and the maximum credit hours permitted by the university.

Prerequisites:
A prerequisite is a course that must be successfully completed before a student can register for another course that depends on it.

Academic Warning:
A student may receive an academic warning if the cumulative GPA falls below the minimum GPA required by the university.

Semester GPA:
The semester GPA is calculated by dividing the total quality points earned during the semester by the total registered credit hours.

Cumulative GPA:
The cumulative GPA represents the student's overall academic performance across all completed semesters.

Credit Hours:
Each academic course has a specific number of credit hours. Credit hours are used when calculating GPA and determining graduation requirements.

Academic Advisor:
The academic advisor helps students understand academic regulations, course requirements, prerequisites, GPA, and academic planning.
